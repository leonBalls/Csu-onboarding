What is the CSU?

The Classified Staff Union represents and protects the interests of hourly employees at UMass Boston

  • ✓ Clerical & Administrative Support Staff
  • ✓ Facilities & Maintenance Staff
  • ✓ Community Service Officers
  • ✓ All hourly positions

🎁 Special Bonuses

  • Grade 17+: $400 one-time bonus
  • Grades 17/18 Step 14: Extra $1,000 bonus (last 2 years)
  • Longevity Bonus: $500/year for 15+ years of service

📅 Vacation Accrual (Full-time)

  • Less than 4 years: 10 days/year
  • 4-9 years: 15 days/year
  • 9-19 years: 20 days/year
  • 19+ years: 25 days/year

Sick Leave Buy-back: Up to 7 days based on usage

💼 Additional Benefits

  • Shift Differential: Extra pay for evening/night shifts
  • Standby Pay: $25/day (updated from $15)
  • Clothing Allowance (CSO): $2,000/year
  • Overtime Meal Allowance: Breakfast $12, Lunch $15, Dinner $25
