🎉 Welcome to CSU

Classified Staff Union

UMass Boston Onboarding

Let's learn about your benefits and rights

What This Means: This onboarding session is designed to help you understand your membership in the Classified Staff Union and all the benefits and protections you receive. As a new member, you're joining a community of workers who are committed to fair wages, safe working conditions, and professional development.

What is the CSU?

The Classified Staff Union represents and protects the interests of hourly employees at UMass Boston

  • ✓ Clerical & Administrative Support Staff
  • ✓ Facilities & Maintenance Staff
  • ✓ Community Service Officers
  • ✓ All hourly positions
Why It Matters: The CSU is your collective voice in the workplace. Through collective bargaining, we negotiate contracts that set your salary, benefits, and working conditions. Union membership means you have legal representation and support if workplace issues arise. Your union steward is your advocate and can help resolve disputes or concerns.

Your Contract

2024-2027

July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2027

This agreement covers your salary, benefits, working conditions, and rights as a CSU member

Why It Matters: Your contract is a legally binding agreement between you and UMass Boston. It protects your rights and guarantees minimum standards for pay, benefits, and working conditions. Everything discussed in this presentation comes from this contract. You can access the full contract document through the HR website for complete details and protections.

💰 Scheduled Salary Increases

Jan 12, 2025

3% Raise

Jul 13, 2025

2% Raise

Jan 11, 2026

2% Raise

Additional raises scheduled through June 2027

Why It Matters: These guaranteed raises mean your income will increase automatically on these dates without requiring any action from you. The first raise is 3%, then subsequent raises are 2%. For example, if you earn $30,000 today, you'll gain $900 in January and $600 every subsequent raise. Check with HR for exact raise amounts based on your current grade and step.

🎁 Special Bonuses

  • Grade 17+: $400 one-time bonus
  • Grades 17/18 Step 14: Extra $1,000 bonus (last 2 years)
  • Longevity Bonus: $500/year for 15+ years of service
Why It Matters: Bonuses reward your loyalty and seniority. Check your grade level with HR to see if you qualify. The longevity bonus recognizes your commitment to UMass Boston and the CSU. These are extra payments on top of your regular salary, helping you achieve financial stability and recognize your value to the organization.

📅 Vacation Accrual (Full-time)

  • Less than 4 years: 10 days/year
  • 4-9 years: 15 days/year
  • 9-19 years: 20 days/year
  • 19+ years: 25 days/year

Sick Leave Buy-back: Up to 7 days based on usage

Why It Matters: Vacation time increases as you build tenure, rewarding loyalty and giving you more flexibility for rest and personal needs. The sick leave buy-back means if you don't use all your sick days, you can convert them to pay at the end of the year. Plan your vacation early with your supervisor to ensure coverage and approval.

💼 Additional Benefits

  • Shift Differential: Extra pay for evening/night shifts
  • Standby Pay: $25/day (updated from $15)
  • Clothing Allowance (CSO): $2,000/year
  • Overtime Meal Allowance: Breakfast $12, Lunch $15, Dinner $25
Why It Matters: These benefits recognize the extra demands of certain positions and schedules. If you work evenings, nights, or weekends, you receive higher pay rates. Standby pay compensates you for being available to respond to emergencies. Meal allowances ensure you're taken care of during long shifts. Check if you're eligible for any of these based on your role.

⚖️ Your Rights as a CSU Member

  • ✓ Fair grievance procedures
  • ✓ Job security protections
  • ✓ Safe working conditions
  • ✓ Equal promotion opportunities
  • ✓ Representation in disputes
Why It Matters: These rights protect you from unfair treatment at work. If you experience discrimination, unsafe conditions, or unjust discipline, you have legal recourse through the union. The grievance process ensures your concerns are heard fairly. You're never alone—your union steward is trained to advocate for you and ensure management follows contractual obligations.
